Reading Guide

How to use this version

  1. Use A− / A+ to adjust the passage and question text from 16 to 36. The font is Be Vietnam Pro; ↺ returns it to 18.
  2. Each passage has its own 15-minute timer. The timer state is saved automatically in this browser. Switching passages pauses the previous timer. When time runs out, you may continue practising.
  3. Turn on Highlight, then select exactly the letters, word, or phrase you want.
  4. Turn on ? Dictionary and click any English word in the reading, questions, or answer choices. The popup shows the Vietnamese meaning and a speaker button for pronunciation. Common lesson vocabulary works offline; other words require an internet connection. Pronunciation depends on voices available in your browser.
  5. Check this passage reports Correct/Incorrect for all 10 Reading Questions and 20 B2 Paraphrase Challenge items without revealing the answer key.

Paragraphs are labelled P1, P2, P3… Each passage contains 10 Reading Questions followed by a 20-item B2 Paraphrase Challenge. Switch between the four tabs; your answers are saved separately for each passage.
